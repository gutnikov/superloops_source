# Choose from options

## Loop

On questions of taste — UI, naming, the shape of an API, the structure of a module — the agent makes 2–3 options.
The person picks one and briefly explains why.

## Getting a good signal

- The options must differ in substance, not cosmetically.
- The explanation of the choice is recorded as a rule, so that next time no choice is needed
  ([correction-becomes-rule](correction-becomes-rule.md)).

## When and how long

During the task or at its end; minutes.

The note gives no primary source: this loop generalizes common practice.
